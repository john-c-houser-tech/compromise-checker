# Sponsoring

Compromise Checker is free and open source, and it stays that way. If it is
useful to you, there are three ways to pay for more of it. Each has one fixed
price.

| | Price | What it pays for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Buy me a coffee](https://buy.stripe.com/bJe6oz3Yp3nmcgwgcA2Ji00) | You choose; $5 is usual | A thank-you |
| [Sponsor a threat pack](#sponsor-a-threat-pack) | $250 | One new threat pack, published for everyone |
| [Sponsor a distribution](#sponsor-a-distribution) | $1,000 | One more Linux distribution supported, and tested for a year |

For a pack or a distribution, **write first**: info@nouriloop.com. You pay
only after we have agreed what will be built.

## Sponsor a threat pack

A [threat pack](packs.md) teaches the tool about one attack: which signs of
it to look for, and when a server was open to it. You name the threat, and
your sponsorship pays for the pack to be written, tested on throwaway servers,
signed and published.

- The threat must be one that gets a pack: it is being exploited, and it
  leaves signs on a server ([which threats get a
  pack](packs.md#which-threats-get-a-pack)). If it doesn't qualify, we say so
  before you pay.
- The pack is published for every user of the tool, under the same licence as
  every other pack. It isn't private to you.
- You are credited by name in the packs repository and in the release notes,
  with a link if you want one. Or stay anonymous.

## Sponsor a distribution

Today the tool checks servers running Red Hat Enterprise Linux 9 or later
(tested on Rocky Linux and AlmaLinux) and Ubuntu 22.04 or later. Your
sponsorship adds one more distribution to that list.

- **What you get:** the collector and the checks made to work on the
  distribution's current releases, and the distribution added to the tests
  that every release must pass, for one year: the proof that the tool writes
  nothing to the server, and the scripted break-ins it must catch.
- **Which distributions:** Debian and SUSE (SUSE Linux Enterprise Server and
  openSUSE Leap) are ready to be sponsored. For any other, ask. Some would
  take far more work than the price covers, and we will say so.
- **Credit:** your name beside the distribution in the README for the year,
  with a link if you want one. Or stay anonymous.
- **After the year:** the distribution stays supported for as long as that is
  practical. Sponsoring it again keeps it in the tests for another year.

## What a sponsorship is not

- **Not a support contract or a warranty.** The tool is provided as is, as
  section 14 of the [security and privacy
  policy](security-and-privacy-policy.md) says.
- **Not a say in what the tool reports.** Sponsors have no influence over
  checks, scores or findings.
- **Not a delivery date.** You get an honest estimate before you pay. If the
  work turns out not to be possible, your money is returned.
- **Not a charitable gift.** You are buying from NouriLoop LLC, a business. A
  payment isn't tax-deductible as a gift, and you get a receipt.
